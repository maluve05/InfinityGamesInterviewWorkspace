## Implementation details
1. **Config (`public/src/config.js`)**:
   - Added `CONFIG.gem = Object.freeze({ floatM: 0.9, skipHeightM: 1.25 })`.
2. **Engine (`public/src/engine.js`)**:
   - In `canSkipGem(state, it, speed)`: checks if the runner's height at the moment of passing the gem's `z` is `>= skipHeightM` (1.25 m). Full jumps (peak ~1.73 m) pass this threshold, while tap jumps (peak ~1.0 m) and grounded runners do not.
   - In `tick`: when passing a gem with `canSkipGem` returning true, the gem is removed from `items` and `{ type: 'skip', color, lane, id }` is emitted without calling `collectGem`. Chain, stumbles, and `chaserStep` remain untouched.
   - In `setupTutorialJump`: positioned the post-barrier gems at `bZ + minGap` and `bZ + minGap + 8`, ensuring they are placed beyond the barrier landing window so a full jump does not skip them.
   - In `spawnAhead`: tracked per-lane barrier locations in `state.lastBarrierByLane`. Enforced landing clearance so no gem can spawn in a barrier's lane until at least `minGapM` beyond that barrier. Prevented thread drift into any barrier's lane during its cooldown window, guaranteeing every row has a ground-collectable thread gem.
3. **Tests (`tests/skip.test.mjs`)**:
   - Added suite covering all 5 required acceptance criteria: grounded collection, full-jump skip without chain breakage or chaser advancement, tap jump collection at peak distance, chain preservation over skipped wrong-color gems, and 5 seeds × 3 minutes top-speed verification of landing room and ground-collectable thread gems.
