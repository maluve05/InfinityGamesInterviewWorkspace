- Input wiring in `public/src/main.js` handles `Space`, `ArrowUp`, and `KeyW` keyup sending `jumpRelease`; `ArrowDown` and `KeyS` sending `drop`; and touch/pointerdrag sending `jump` on drag up followed by `jumpRelease` on pointerup.
