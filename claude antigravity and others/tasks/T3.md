## Why
Owner's playtest: "not enough space between jumps … learn from the chrome dinosaur game". Today the jump is a fixed 650 ms parabola. Barriers can also land on consecutive 9 m rows in one lane, so you land straight into the next one. At 40 m/s you can't jump twice in time.

## Rules to build (all numbers go in `CONFIG`, nothing hard-coded)
1. **Physics jump in metres and seconds.** Replace the parabola with velocity and gravity, still deterministic in `tick`.
   - `CONFIG.jump = { velocity, gravity, cutVelocity, dropGravityFactor, clearHeight }`. Start from about `velocity 9 m/s`, `gravity 30 m/s²`, which gives a full jump of about 0.6 s and 1.35 m high. Tune them.
   - Keep `state.jumpMs` meaning "ms since take-off, or null when grounded". Add `state.jumpY` (metres) and `state.jumpV` (m/s).
   - `jumpHeight(state)` keeps its signature and returns `state.jumpY` (0 when grounded). The view depends on it.
2. **Variable height (dino).** New action `{ type: 'jumpRelease' }`. If it arrives while you are still rising faster than `cutVelocity`, set the velocity to `cutVelocity`. That gives a short hop. A full hold gives the full jump.
3. **Fast fall (dino).** New action `{ type: 'drop' }`. While airborne, gravity × `dropGravityFactor` (about 3) until landing. On the ground it does nothing.
4. **Barrier height.** `CONFIG.barrier = { heightM: 0.9, lengthM: 1.2 }`. The runner clears a barrier when `jumpY >= barrier.heightM` at any moment while inside the barrier's `lengthM` (not only at one point). A short hop must still clear a barrier, but only just.
5. **Barrier spacing (dino gap rule).** At spawn time, a barrier may not be placed less than `minGapM` ahead of the previous barrier, in any lane:
   `minGapM = speedAtThatZ × fullJumpAirtime × CONFIG.barrierGap.coefficient + CONFIG.barrierGap.landingBufferM`. Use coefficient ≈ 1.6 and buffer ≈ 4 m. Estimate the speed at that z from `baseSpeed` and the distance. A rough estimate is fine, but the rule must hold at max speed.
   Keep everything that exists now: no barriers before `barriersFromM`, never on the thread lane, never a fully blocked row.
6. **Input wiring (main.js only).** Space/↑/W keydown → `jump` (as now). Keyup of those keys → `jumpRelease`. ArrowDown/S keydown → `drop`. Pointer: swipe/drag up stays `jump`; pointerup after a jump swipe sends `jumpRelease`, so touch gets the full jump on a slow drag. Don't change anything else in main.js.
7. Events stay the same: `jump`, `land`, `vault`, `stumble {cause:'hit'}`. Add `{ type: 'drop' }` when a fast fall starts.

## Tests (tests/jump.test.mjs)
- A held jump peaks at least 1.6× higher than a tap (jump then `jumpRelease` 1 tick later).
- A tap still clears a barrier of `heightM`. A grounded runner hits it.
- `drop` mid-air lands sooner than no drop.
- At top speed (`CONFIG.speed.max`), in 5 seeds × 3 minutes of generated track, the gap between consecutive barriers along the track is always ≥ the `minGapM` rule. A bot that jumps every barrier in its lane (on the last frame where a full jump still clears it) never stumbles.
- Determinism: same seed and same inputs give the same `summary`.
