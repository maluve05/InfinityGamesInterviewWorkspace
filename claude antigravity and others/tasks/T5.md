## The rule (owner)
"stumbling/wrong colour gets it one step closer to you (max 3 before you get caught) and a 6 combo earns you distance (max 3)" … "dont add any banking of shields or it becomes too easy".

## Contract (Claude's view relies on exactly this)
- `CONFIG.chaser = { steps: 3 }`. Remove `startGapM`, `maxGapM`, `paceStart`, `pacePerSec`, `paceMax`, `chain.stepBackPerStepM` and `chain.maxStepBackM`, along with their logic.
- `state.chaserStep`: integer 0–3, starts at 0.
- **Mistake** = breaking a 1–2 chain (`break` event) or hitting a barrier (`stumble {cause:'hit'}`). Each one: `chaserStep += 1` and emit `{ type: 'chaser', step, dir: 1 }`. Reaching `steps`: `over = true`, `cause = 'caught'`, emit `caught` as today.
- Cashing a 3–5 chain by switching colour is **not** a mistake. It changes nothing on the chaser.
- **6-combo** (the `match` with `max: true`): if `chaserStep > 0`, `chaserStep -= 1` and emit `{ type: 'chaser', step, dir: -1 }`. At 0 nothing happens. No banking.
- The stumble slowdown stays (it's feel), but it no longer moves the chaser.
- Remove `state.gap` and `chaserPace`. Update `summary()`: replace any gap field with `chaserStep`. main.js reads `state.gap` once for telemetry (`resource_left`). Leave main.js alone; Claude will switch it to `chaserStep`.
- Tutorial (T4): while the tutorial runs, mistakes don't move the chaser. The `storm` step shows the storm by setting `chaserStep = 1` for its duration, then resets it to 0 on `done`.

## Tests (tests/chaser.test.mjs)
- Starts at 0. Three mistakes in any mix (break, hit) → caught on the third, with `chaser` events `step 1, 2` before `caught`.
- A cashed 3–5 chain doesn't change `chaserStep`.
- Mistake, mistake, 6-combo → step 1. A 6-combo at step 0 stays at 0 (no banking), and a later mistake → step 1.
- An idle runner who never makes a mistake isn't caught in 60 s (on a quiet track with no items).
- Tutorial: mistakes during the tutorial don't move the chaser; after `done`, `chaserStep` is 0.
