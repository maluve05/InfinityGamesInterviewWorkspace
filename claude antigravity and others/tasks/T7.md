## Owner's report
"someone failing the tutorial results in a failed state of the game where you run forever" (screenshot: stuck on JUMP with an empty road).
Root cause (Claude's reading): `setupTutorialJump` puts one barrier in the runner's current lane and waits for `vault` or a hit. A lane change passes it with neither, and nothing respawns it. The `lanes` and `chain` steps have the same shape: they only advance or repeat on specific events.

## Rules
1. **Jump step:** the tutorial barrier spans **all three lanes** (three barrier items at the same z, tutorial only), so jumping is the only way through. Hitting it repeats the step (as now).
2. **Any step's pattern passed without completing:** once the runner is more than 6 m past the step's last authored item, respawn that step's pattern ahead (same as a miss).
3. **Hard limits:** each step gives up after `CONFIG.tutorial.stepMaxMs` (12000) and advances to the next step anyway. The whole tutorial ends (`done`) after `CONFIG.tutorial.maxMs` (45000) at the latest. On `done` everything hands over as today.
4. The track is never empty for more than 2 s during the tutorial, except the planned `storm` pause.
5. Keep the event contract: `{type:'tutorial', step}` on every change, `targetLane` for the lanes and chain steps.

## Tests (tests/tutorial.test.mjs)
- A runner who changes lane at the jump step (dodging) still faces a full-width barrier and can only pass by jumping or hitting it.
- A runner who does nothing at all reaches `done` within 45 s of game time.
- A runner who mashes left/right/jump randomly (seeded) reaches `done` within 45 s.
- A runner who misses the chain pattern sees it respawn (items ahead again within 2 s).
- Existing tutorial tests still pass.
