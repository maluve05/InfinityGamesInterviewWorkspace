## Owner's report
"jumping doesnt seem to work because it still collects the item you were trying to jump over, i think this logic error is the reason why jumps werent spaced out correctly either."
Root cause: in `tick`, a gem in the runner's lane is collected whenever the runner passes its z, at any height. The T3 spec (Claude's) never said otherwise.

## Rules
1. `CONFIG.gem = { floatM: 0.9, skipHeightM: 1.25 }`. Gems float at `floatM` (the view already draws them there). A runner whose `jumpY >= skipHeightM` at the moment it passes a gem's z **skips** it: the gem is not collected, stays out of the chain, and the engine emits `{ type: 'skip', color, lane, id }`. The gem is removed from `items`.
   - A full jump (peak about 1.73 m) clears a gem. A tap (peak about 1.0 m) doesn't, so the tap stays the barrier hop.
   - Skipping isn't a mistake: no break, no stumble, no chaser step.
2. **Room to land.** After a barrier, the next row in that barrier's lane may hold a gem only if it's at least `minGapM` (the T3 rule at that speed) beyond the barrier. Rows in the other lanes are unchanged. Keep every existing rule: the thread is always reachable, no fully blocked row, the T3 barrier gap, no barriers before `barriersFromM`.
3. The thread (the guaranteed chain path) must stay collectable on the ground. Never require a jump to keep the thread.
4. Tutorial: the `jump` step's gems after the barrier start at least `minGapM` past it, so a full jump over the barrier doesn't skip them.
5. Determinism unchanged.

## Tests (tests/skip.test.mjs)
- A grounded runner collects a gem in its lane (as now).
- A runner at full-jump height passing a gem: no `collect`, a `skip` event, chain unchanged, no stumble, `chaserStep` unchanged.
- A tap (jump + immediate `jumpRelease`) does NOT skip a gem placed at the tap's peak distance.
- Chain survives a skip: collect ember ×2, skip a tide gem by jumping, collect ember → chain is ember ×3.
- Over 5 seeds × 3 minutes of generated track at top speed: no gem in a barrier's lane sits closer than `minGapM` after that barrier, and every row still has a ground-collectable thread gem.
