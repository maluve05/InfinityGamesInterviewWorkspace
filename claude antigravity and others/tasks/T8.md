## Owner's report (16:09)
"the spacing between gems still seems to be too close and jumping still doesnt work because i still collect things from whatever i intended to jump (the log gets jumped over without issue)" … "make the animation complete much much faster".

Why T6 isn't enough (Claude's reading): a gem is skipped only while `jumpY >= skipHeightM` (1.25 m). A full jump is above that for only about 0.36 s of its 0.68 s, about 5 m of road at base speed, so most real jumps still collect. The log works because its clear height is 0.9 m.

## Rules
1. **Airborne = no pickup.** While `jumpY > CONFIG.gem.skipHeightM`, gems in the lane are skipped (the `skip` event as in T6). Set `skipHeightM: 0.25`, so any jump past its first instant skips. Remove the "tap doesn't skip" behaviour: any jump skips.
2. **Wider spacing:** `spawn.rowSpacingM` 9 → 15. Retune whatever depended on 9 m (thread length, `gemChance` if needed) so that a 3+ chain is still always on offer and the colour-unlock pace stays about the same (3,000 points reachable within about 2 minutes for a decent player). Keep the T3 and T6 gap rules and the tutorial's spacing (scale `tutorial.rowSpacingM` to 18).
3. **Snappier jump, same height:** keep a full-jump peak of about 1.6–1.75 m but cut the airtime about 30% (for example `velocity` ≈ 12.5, `gravity` ≈ 46). Retune `cutVelocity` so a tap is still noticeably lower. The barrier clear rule (0.9 m) must still hold for a tap.
4. Everything else unchanged: determinism, thread on the ground, no fully blocked row.

## Tests
- A jump started 1 m before a gem in the lane skips it (no `collect`, a `skip`, chain unchanged). Also when started 4 m before it.
- A grounded runner still collects.
- A full-jump airtime ≤ 0.5 s and peak between 1.6 and 1.75 m. A tap still clears a 0.9 m barrier.
- Generated rows are ≥ 15 m apart, and a chain-following bot still reaches 1,000 points in under 60 s on 3 seeds (report the times).
