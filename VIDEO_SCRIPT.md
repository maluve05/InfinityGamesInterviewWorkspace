> - Browser open to https://infinity.chitswa.com in a clean desktop window (1440×900 or 1080p).
> - Audio turned ON (sound effects and soundtrack are part of the feel).
> - Microphone checked; close unrelated tabs and notification badges.
> - Have `context/charts/` or Subway Surfers store screenshot handy for the 1:00 transition.

### [0:00 – 1:00] Part 1: Live Demo & Core Loop

**(On screen: https://infinity.chitswa.com. Press Space or Click to Start. Play through the short tutorial into the main run.)**

> **What to Say:**
> "Hi everyone, this is **Chroma Rush**, a three-lane browser runner built from scratch using AI assistance for the Infinity Games assessment.
>
> In one sentence: Chroma Rush combines the reflexive lane-dodging of an endless runner with the tactical pattern recognition of a match-3 color chain.
>
> As you see on screen, your character sprints forward down three lanes. Gems of different colors appear ahead. Collecting gems of the same color builds your chain: 3 matching gems score 300 points, 4 score 600, 5 score 900, and a 6-chain automatically bursts for 1,200 points, which pushes back the shadowy storm chaser right behind us.
>
> But if you hit a different color before reaching 3 gems, your chain breaks, you stumble, and the storm gains a step. Three stumbles and you're caught! 
>
> Notice the jump mechanic: when a barrier or an unwanted gem is in your lane, you can tap or hold jump to vault cleanly over it, keeping your active color streak alive without breaking your chain."

### [1:00 – 1:40] Part 2: Inspiration & The Twist

**(On screen: Show the Google Play top-chart screenshot from `context/charts/` or Subway Surfers store page, then bring Chroma Rush back into view.)**

> **What to Say:**
> "The prototype was inspired directly by **Subway Surfers**, which currently sits at **#23 on the Google Play Top Free charts**. 
>
> While Subway Surfers perfected the feel of 3-lane dodging, traditional runners often suffer from repetitive swiping where coins are just passive background noise.
>
> Our creative twist for Infinity Games was asking: *'Can we turn mindless dodging into a meaningful color puzzle at high speed?'*
>
> By replacing coin collecting with match-3 color combos, every single lane-switch becomes an active risk/reward choice. You aren't just dodging obstacles—you are actively reading the road 30 meters ahead, calculating whether to jump a log to preserve a 5-streak or dodge safely into an open lane."

### [1:40 – 2:40] Part 3: Scale or Kill Verdict

**(On screen: Play a fast, high-speed run, demonstrating a high chain combo, a 6-burst with slow-mo, and the dusk atmosphere change.)**

> **What to Say:**
> "If this were my prototype at Infinity Games, my verdict is an unequivocal **SCALE**.
>
> Here is why:
> 1. **Core Loop Validation:** The tension works. The combination of twitch reflexes and color filtering creates genuine flow. In our playtests, once players grasp that jumping lets them leap barriers to save their streak, they immediately hit 'Play Again' upon losing.
> 2. **Production Efficiency:** The entire game runs on zero-build, static Canvas 2D and Web Audio. It loads in under 300 milliseconds with zero compilation overhead, making it ideal for instant web portals and high-conversion playable ads.
> 3. **Market & UA Fit:** It merges two of the highest grossing mobile genres in history: endless runners and match-3 cascades. For user acquisition, this gives marketing teams virtually endless hooks for high-CTR video creatives showing dramatic close calls and satisfying 6-combos.
>
> Of course, four hours of internal testing is an encouraging signal, not definitive proof. Our immediate next step would be a 500-player mobile cohort test on touch devices to validate Day 1 retention targeting 38%."
