# Walkthrough video (~3 minutes)

Not a recording of the session — a short walkthrough of what you built. It must
show the prototype running, say **which top-chart game inspired it**, and give a
**scale it or kill it** verdict with reasons. Record after the 17:00 freeze; one
take; share as a link (Loom, Google Drive, unlisted YouTube or your own domain)
that opens without an account. 1080p.

## Script

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | **Demo.** The live URL, then a full round: start → play → end → restart, with sound on | What it is in one sentence; the core loop and the one question it tests; the feel decisions |
| 1:00–1:40 | **Inspiration.** The top-chart game (store page or a screenshot), then your version side by side | Which game, where it ranks, its core mechanic, what you kept, and the twist that fits a relaxing, minimal catalogue |
| 1:40–2:40 | **Scale or kill.** Back on the game; `npm run metrics` output if useful | A clear verdict: fun, fit, market, cost, risk; the next 1–3 tests or why to stop; say plainly that your playtest data is a signal, not evidence |
| 2:40–3:00 | **Process.** Figma directions → live screen; `TASKS.md` | Claude as lead + Gemini worker, what you changed by hand, where the full prompt log is |

## Checklist

- [ ] Close unrelated tabs and notifications; hide `.env` and any keys.
- [ ] Browser window at a normal desktop size; zoom so the game is readable.
- [ ] Sound on in the game (it's part of the feel); mic level checked.
- [ ] Open the share link in a private window to confirm it plays without an account.
