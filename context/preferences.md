# Working preferences (owner)

- **Looks over mechanics.** "I would much rather have a game that looks great, to
  the point where someone would think 'wow, if he had just a little bit of extra
  time it'd be so great and fun', than a game that has all working mechanics but
  looks and feels terrible and you feel like you'd need to overhaul the entire thing."
- **Design first, in Figma.** Work out what the best output looks like before
  building. The owner can refine designs by hand in Figma or by prompting.
- **AI in collaboration with the owner.** The owner may edit or change anything;
  say honestly what was done by hand.
- **Claude owns UI.** Gemini (Antigravity) does only bounded, non-visual worker tasks.
- **Desktop browser first.** Mouse and keyboard; touch only if it comes free.
- **Timeline on the day:** complete prototype by two hours in, polish for one hour,
  freeze, then package; the last hour is buffer for anything that goes wrong.
- **Honesty:** the assessors value honesty over polish.
- **Commits:** no AI attribution lines.
- **Decisions:** use grilling rounds — numbered questions, each with a recommended
  answer, so "yes" accepts it; the owner answers only what they disagree with.
